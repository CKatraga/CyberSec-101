## Quick concept-quiz bank

Pull a few each week:

- What's the difference between a vulnerability, a threat, and a risk?
- What does `chmod 640` mean, digit by digit?
- Walk me through what happens when you type a URL and press Enter.
- What's an open vs. closed vs. filtered port?
- Hashing vs. encryption vs. encoding — when do you use each?
- Why salt a password hash?
- What is a SQL injection, and what's the one-line fix?
- What does TLS actually protect, and what does it *not*?
- Explain least privilege with an example.
- What's the CIA triad? Give a real breach that broke each one.
