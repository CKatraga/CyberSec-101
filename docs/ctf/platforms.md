# Online practice platforms

Once you're comfortable, these are where you build real, demonstrable skill. All have generous free tiers; several give **student discounts** — use your AUM email.

## Guided, beginner-friendly

- **[TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com)** — the best on-ramp. Guided "rooms" with a browser-based Kali and targets, so you need no local setup. Follow the **Pre Security** and **Jr Penetration Tester** paths. 🎓 *Student discount available (~20–30% off) — verify with your AUM email or via Student Beans.*
- **[picoCTF](https://picoctf.org)** — free CTF challenges from Carnegie Mellon, aimed at beginners. Great for Module 11.
- **[OverTheWire — Bandit](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/)** — free Linux/SSH wargame; a perfect companion to Module 1.

## Hands-on labs

- **[PortSwigger Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security)** — *free*, world-class web-hacking labs (used in Module 6).
- **[Hack The Box Academy](https://academy.hackthebox.com)** — structured cybersecurity courses. 🎓 *Student subscription available if your school domain is registered — link your AUM email.*
- **[Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com)** — more challenging boxes; move here after TryHackMe.

## Blue-team / defense

- **[Blue Team Labs Online](https://blueteamlabs.online)** and **[LetsDefend](https://letsdefend.io)** — if you discover you like *defending* more than attacking (SOC analyst path).

!!! tip "Suggested order"
    OverTheWire Bandit → TryHackMe (Pre Security → Jr Pentester) → PortSwigger labs → Hack The Box. Do picoCTF anytime for fun.
