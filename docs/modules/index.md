# Modules overview

Eleven modules, in order. Each one is the same shape:

- **🎥 Watch** — 1–3 curated videos from the best free instructors on YouTube.
- **🛠️ Do** — a hands-on lab you complete in your workspace.
- **🏁 Capture** — a flag, script, or screenshot that proves you did it.
- **✅ Check-in** — what to show Pranith.

Work top to bottom. Don't skip the labs — watching alone won't stick for you, and you already know that about yourself.
