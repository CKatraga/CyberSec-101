# M4 · Security+ Foundations <span class="week-pill">Weeks 6–7</span>

**Goal:** get the shared vocabulary of the whole field — CIA triad, threats vs. vulnerabilities vs. risk, controls, authentication, common attacks. This is also the exact syllabus of the **CompTIA Security+** certification, so this module doubles as cert prep.

*Reinforces: general cybersecurity core; sets up [certifications](../certs.md).*

## 🎥 Watch — two great options

**Option A — Harvard CS50 Cybersecurity (David Malan).** The most engaging intro out there; makes hard ideas click. Great if you want *understanding*.

    Watch **CS50 Cybersecurity** end-to-end for genuine understanding, then use **Professor Messer** as your structured review before the Security+ exam. Messer's free [course notes and practice exams](https://www.professormesser.com/) are excellent.

## 🛠️ Do

- Make a one-page mind-map of the **five Security+ domains** (General Security Concepts; Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations; Security Architecture; Security Operations; Program Management & Oversight).
- For each of these, write one real-world example in your own words: phishing, ransomware, MITM, privilege escalation, social engineering, zero-day.
- Take **one free Messer practice exam** and record your score. Re-take after review.
