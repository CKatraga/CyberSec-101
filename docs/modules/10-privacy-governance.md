## 🛠️ Do

This module is more reading/thinking than terminal work — that's the reality of GRC.

1. **Frameworks:** skim the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework** (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) and write one control you'd put in each function for a small company.
2. **Privacy law:** in your own words, contrast **GDPR** and a US approach (e.g., **HIPAA/CCPA**) — who they protect and what they require.
3. **Vulnerability management (hands-on):** revisit the **CVE** and **NVD** workflow from your AUM Report 2/11 — look up a recent CVE, read its CVSS score, and explain what the score means for prioritization.
4. **IAM & Zero Trust:** using your Report 5/6/9 labs as reference, write the principles of **least privilege** and **"never trust, always verify"** and give an example of each.
